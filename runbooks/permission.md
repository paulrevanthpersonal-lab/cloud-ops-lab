# Permission Denied

1. Capture the principal, resource, attempted action, timestamp, and correlation ID.
2. Confirm authentication succeeded before changing authorization.
3. Review direct and inherited role assignments at each scope.
4. Compare the required data action with the assigned role definition.
5. Apply the least-privileged role at the narrowest correct scope.
6. Allow for propagation, retest, and record effective access.
7. Remove temporary elevation after verification.
